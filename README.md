# COSMIC — Solar System Field Guide

An accessible, responsive, lightweight astronomy guide featuring all eight Solar System planets, NASA mission imagery, interactive planet facts, a five-question quiz, and an evidence-aware look at exoplanet Kepler-442 b.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step or runtime dependencies are required. The typefaces and NASA images load from their respective public websites.

## Features

- Select among eight planets for concise facts, average distance from the Sun, orbital period, and a NASA source link.
- Five-question interactive quiz with answer feedback, score, and restart.
- Kepler-442 b profile clearly distinguishes a habitable-zone orbit from confirmed habitability or life. The star/planet graphic is labeled as an illustration, not a photograph.
- Responsive navigation, keyboard-operable controls, reduced-motion support, and descriptive image text.

## Image and science sources

Planet images are loaded from NASA's public image asset library and link to NASA Solar System Exploration planet pages. Image credits are shown in the explorer. Planetary images may be mosaics, composites, enhanced, or color-processed; the page does not imply they are scale comparisons.

Kepler-442 b references: [NASA Exoplanet Catalog](https://science.nasa.gov/exoplanet-catalog/kepler-442-b/) and [NASA Exoplanet Archive](https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/overview/Kepler-442%20b). Approximate catalog values may change with improved measurements. A habitable-zone orbit does not establish a planet's atmosphere, surface water, habitability, or life. “Superhabitable” is a theoretical term, not a confirmed status for Kepler-442 b or any known exoplanet. The theoretical discussion links to the peer-reviewed paper [A Catalog of Habitable Exoplanets](https://www.liebertpub.com/doi/10.1089/ast.2019.2161).

NASA imagery and links do not imply NASA's endorsement of COSMIC.
